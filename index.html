Before you start

Can I use Focus Habitat as a Pomodoro timer?

Yes. Set a 25-minute focus session, take a 5-minute break, and start the next session yourself. You can also choose a different session length.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open Focus Habitat, choose your session length and start focusing.

Is Habitat Plus required?

No. The focus timer, your history and everything you earn stay free. Plus adds optional ways to personalise your experience.
