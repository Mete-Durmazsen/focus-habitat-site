How a session works

Before · 0:00

Pick a length. Name it, or don't.

Choose a session from 5 to 120 minutes, or use a quick preset. Add a label like Thesis or Guitar if you want the time to grow into a chapter. Then tap Start Focus.

During · 12:40 left

The room dims. Nothing asks for you.

A quiet room tone, a countdown, and Mino working beside you. Pause, take a note, or step away; the timer keeps time from real timestamps, even if the app closes.

After · 25:00 done

A short, kind ending.

See what the session added, a leaf for the bowl and a little Star Dust, then go. No scores, no streak counter, no push to keep going.

The leaf bowl

One session, one leaf. Thirty-five make a season.

On a shelf in your room sits a small bowl. Every credited session adds a leaf. When it holds 35, the season turns and the bowl starts again, while every leaf stays counted in your history.

Questions

Good to know

Is Focus Habitat free?

Yes. Focusing, chapters, the room and everything you earn are free. Habitat Plus is an optional membership with extra sounds, colours and designs.

What if I skip a few days?

Nothing happens. There are no streaks and nothing decays. Your room and chapters wait exactly where you left them.

Does the timer keep going if I leave the app?

Yes. Sessions are measured from real timestamps, so they stay accurate in the background and after the app closes. A notification tells you when time is up.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. You can export a backup file at any time.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
